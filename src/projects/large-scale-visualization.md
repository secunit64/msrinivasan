## The problem

Direct numerical simulations of turbulent combustion produce datasets too large for a conventional desktop workflow. Scientists need to examine turbulence structures at different scales while preserving access to the underlying simulation data.

## My contribution

At KAUST's Visualization Core Lab, I worked with domain scientists to create distributed visualization workflows using the Shaheen II supercomputer, a high-throughput network connection, and a GPU visualization cluster.

The work combined parallel data processing with streaming so researchers could explore simulation output interactively. Related research investigated query-driven exploration and image-based techniques for large datasets.

## Mentoring and research

I supervised master's research on image-based exploration of pathline fields and multivariable isosurfaces. These projects investigated ways to make large scientific datasets easier to explore through carefully chosen representations.
