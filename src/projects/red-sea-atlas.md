## The problem

Ocean and atmospheric researchers work with data from numerical simulations and observations. Understanding the Red Sea requires comparing these sources across variables, locations, and time. The analysis tasks range from nutrient transport to marine heat waves and wind-energy patterns.

## My contribution

At KAUST, my lab and I collaborated with Ibrahim Hoteit's group to design custom visual analytics tools for these tasks. RedSeaAtlas brought multivariate ocean and atmospheric data into a common environment for exploration.

Our work connected visualization expertise with the questions domain scientists needed to ask. The tool supported analysis of nutrient flows, extreme events, and regional wind patterns.

## Research context

Nutrient transport affects the availability of food for marine life. Sustained marine heat waves can contribute to coral bleaching. Wind patterns inform the placement and operation of renewable-energy infrastructure. Each task requires a different view of the same changing environment.
