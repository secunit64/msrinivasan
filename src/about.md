# About me

<div class="article">
<p class="lede">I'm Madhu Srinivasan, a researcher working at the intersection of computer graphics, generative AI, and scientific visualization.</p>

I am Principal Research Staff at AMD Research. My current interests include generative world models, controllable generation, and efficient real-time inference.

Before AMD, I was Lead Scientist for Visualization and Machine Learning at HPE Cray. Earlier, I helped establish KAUST's Visualization Core Lab, working with researchers in climate science, combustion, marine ecology, and neuroscience. That work involved distributed graphics, immersive virtual reality, and the practical task of making large datasets useful to scientists.
